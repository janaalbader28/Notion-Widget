# ✦ Notion Widgets

A small collection of widgets designed to make Notion pages a little more useful and personal, especially for things I couldn't find available online. ♡

## What's Inside

🐈‍⬛ **Mood Tracker**  
A simple pixel-style mood tracker for Notion.

⏳ **Holiday Countdown**  
A pixel-style countdown to upcoming holidays, reading weeks, and bank holidays.

More widgets coming soon. ✦

## Embed

You can embed the widgets directly into Notion or other projects.

**Mood Tracker:**  
[Open Mood Tracker](https://notion-mode-tracker.vercel.app/)

**Holiday Countdown:**  
[Open Holiday Countdown](https://notion-holiday-countdown.vercel.app/)

Just copy the link and add it using `/embed` in Notion.
